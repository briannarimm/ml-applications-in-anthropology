# Credits

## Data

All categorical data comes from [D-PLACE](https://d-place.org). All location data comes from [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org).

The data uses these D-PLACE variables:

* [SCCS6](https://d-place.org/parameters/SCCS6) and [SCCS10](https://d-place.org/parameters/SCCS10): Murdock, G. P., & Morrow, D. O. (1970). Subsistence economy and supportive practices: Cross-cultural codes 1. *Ethnology*, 9(3), 302-330.
* [SCCS238](https://d-place.org/parameters/SCCS238): Murdock (1962-1971); Gray (1999).
* [SCCS2002](https://d-place.org/parameters/SCCS2002): Rosenblatt, P. C., Walsh, R. P., & Jackson, D. A. (2011). Grief and mourning codes. *World Cultures eJournal*, 18(2).

Glottolog: Hammarström, Harald & Forkel, Robert & Haspelmath, Martin & Bank, Sebastian. 2021. Glottolog 4.4. Leipzig: Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.4761960> (accessed 2021-10-24).
